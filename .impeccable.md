## Design Context

### Users
Kids (roughly 5–12) and their parents playing a 7-day family photo scavenger hunt over Thanksgiving week. Kids shoot on small DSLR cameras or phones and upload at the end of each day from a computer or phone. Several kids compete on one family scoreboard.

### Brand Personality
Sticker-book, sunny, scrappy. Should feel like a homemade activity book come to life: celebratory, encouraging, never stressful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme on warm cream "paper" with a dot grid. Thick ink outlines with hard offset shadows (stickers), slight tilts. Each day has its own bright color (green, blue, gold, pumpkin, berry, grape, caramel) taken from the printable PDF. Display font: Bagel Fat One. Body: Lexend (designed for reading ease).

### Design Principles
1. Big, forgiving touch targets: small hands, tablets, phones.
2. Every checkmark is a small celebration (stamp, +points pop, confetti), with reduced-motion respected.
3. The end-of-day photo upload must be fast: many photos at once, tap photo → tap mission.
4. Scores are always visible and derived, never typed in.
5. Grown-up controls (special points, awards, backups) stay out of kids' way.
